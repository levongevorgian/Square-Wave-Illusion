# Reading and technical references

## Vision science literature

1. Leguire, L. E., Blake, R., and Sloane, M. E. (1981). A Novel Illusion of Bars Made from Triangles. *Science*, 212, 1172–1175. The original report describes alternation between the triangle-wave and illusory bar percept and considers physical, afterimage, and phase-related accounts. It reports adaptation results consistent with a central contribution.
2. Wildzunas, R. M., and May, J. G. (1992). Phase shifts and the square-wave illusion. *Spatial Vision*, 6, 209–220. The experiments connect small shifts of harmonic phase with the apparent direction of illumination and consider experience-related discrimination and drift comparisons.
3. May, J. G. (1994). Stimulus manipulations that reduce the square-wave illusion. *Spatial Vision*, 8, 329–340. The experiments study phase alternation and local contrast manipulations in relation to line/edge mechanisms and illusion predominance.

These papers motivate the project but are not simulated in full. The code does not reproduce their observers, apparatus, or behavioral datasets. Their PDFs are not redistributed in this repository.

## Technical resources

- [Wolfram MathWorld: Sphere Point Picking](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/SpherePointPicking.html); explains sphere sampling and the pole-bias issue. This implementation uses deterministic Fibonacci quadrature rather than random point picking.
- [SciPy: vonmises_fisher](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.vonmises_fisher.html), directional distribution definition and concentration parameter.
- [SciPy: differential_evolution](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.differential_evolution.html), bounded global-search algorithm used here.
- [SciPy: Powell minimization](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.minimize-powell.html), local derivative-free refinement used here.

Technical links are supplied for the definitions and tools, not as evidence for the project's numerical findings. Those findings are generated by the included code and saved in results/optimization/.
